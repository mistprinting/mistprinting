## Suggested campaign setup

- Use the website quote form as the destination.
- Test each creative in its own ad so results are easy to compare.
- Start with Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, and the surrounding Treasure Valley for the local campaign.
- Keep the broad full-color ad for mixed audiences. Use the group-order ad for teams and events, and the sticker ad for business and organization interests.
- Track quote-form starts and completed submissions if analytics are available.
