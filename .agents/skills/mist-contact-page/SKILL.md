# Mist Contact Page

## Purpose

Make starting a quote quick, clear, and low-friction.

## Required content

- The primary CTA opens the owner-provided Notion form.
- Until the URL is provided, show `Quote form coming soon` and never invent a destination.
- Ask visitors to prepare item type, approximate quantity, desired date, delivery or pickup preference, and artwork if available.
- Display confirmed email, phone, address, and hours only after the owner supplies them.
- Link to `/privacy` near the form handoff.
- Provide service and gallery links for visitors still deciding.
