# Mist Home Page

## Purpose

Convert local and national visitors into quote requests while proving print quality and high-volume capability within the first two sections.

## Required order

1. Header with simple navigation and quote CTA.
2. Hero: `Custom printing built to show up.`
3. Proof strip: local service, high-volume capability, nationwide shipping, clear process.
4. Services: DTF apparel, stickers and decals, bulk and event orders.
5. Featured real work.
6. Use cases: businesses, teams and sporting events, churches, reunions, schools, and community groups.
7. Large-order message: `Big order? Bring it on.`
8. Four-step process.
9. Service-area summary.
10. Visible FAQ.
11. Final quote CTA and footer.

## Hero contract

- Do not use eyebrow text or small uppercase section labels anywhere on the homepage.
- Headline: `Custom printing built to show up.`
- Supporting message must mention DTF apparel, stickers, decals, Treasure Valley service, and nationwide shipping.
- Primary CTA: `Start your quote`
- The primary CTA opens the confirmed external Notion quote form in a new tab.
- Secondary CTA: `See recent work`
- Use supplied work photography as the dominant visual.

## SEO contract

- Primary intent: custom DTF printing in Boise and the Treasure Valley.
- Secondary intents: custom apparel printing, sporting event apparel printing, family reunion shirts, church apparel, stickers, decals, bulk printing, nationwide shipping.
- Do not repeat city names unnaturally or use a meta-keywords tag.
