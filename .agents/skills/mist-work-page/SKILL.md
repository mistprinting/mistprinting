# Mist Work Page

## Purpose

Turn real finished work into visual proof of detail, color, variety, and production capacity.

## Gallery rules

- Use supplied Mist Printing project media unless the owner provides more.
- Organize into `Apparel`, `Stickers & decals`, and `Behind the scenes` when supported by the media.
- Use descriptive filenames, honest captions, and useful alt text. Do not identify a client unless confirmed.
- Load images responsively and lazily below the first row.
- Give every video a poster, `preload="none"`, visible controls, and no autoplay audio.
- Keep the homepage selection curated; the complete collection belongs here.
- Include a quote CTA after the gallery.
