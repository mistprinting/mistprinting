# Mist Services Page

## Purpose

Help customers determine whether Mist can produce their order and move qualified visitors to the quote form.

## Required content

- DTF apparel: detailed full-color artwork applied with heat and pressure across many garment colors and practical order sizes.
- Stickers and decals: custom shapes and full-color brand, event, and community applications.
- Bulk and event printing: businesses, sports, churches, schools, reunions, fundraisers, and events.
- Process: share idea, confirm details, approve artwork, print, then ship or arrange pickup.
- Transparent limitation: Mist does not offer embroidery yet.
- Do not claim garment brands, materials, minimums, pricing, or turnaround until confirmed.

## Conversion contract

Use quote CTAs that open the confirmed external Notion form, including after the service comparison and at the end. Link proof to `/our-work`.
