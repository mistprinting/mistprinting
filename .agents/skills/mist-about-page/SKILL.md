# Mist About Page

## Purpose

Build trust in Mist Printing as an approachable local production partner capable of serving organizations and shipping nationally.

## Content rules

- Anchor the story in Boise and the Treasure Valley without inventing a founding year, owner biography, team size, equipment list, or physical address.
- Emphasize clear communication, bold full-color work, reliable coordination, and pride in the result.
- Balance local service with nationwide fulfillment.
- Use real behind-the-scenes media when available.
- End with links to `/our-work` and `/contact`.
